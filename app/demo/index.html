🌍 Polygon Selection Management Workflow

Interactive demonstration of climate data analysis: from GeoTIFF upload to CZML visualisation

Workflow Overview

This comprehensive workflow demonstrates the complete pipeline for selecting and analysing climate data regions. Each step builds upon the previous one, culminating in exportable CZML visualisations for Cesium.
